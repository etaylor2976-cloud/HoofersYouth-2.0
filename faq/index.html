Eligibility & experience

No. Beginner Sailing and Windsurfing welcome first-time sailors. Advanced Sailing is designed for youth who have sailed before.

Our current programs are designed for youth ages 10–18. We group instruction by readiness and experience when possible.

02

Safety

Properly fitted life jackets, trained instructors, coach boats, clear boundaries, and weather-aware plans are standard every day.

Sailors should be comfortable around water. Life jackets remain on during on-water activities, and families can contact us about specific comfort or support needs.

03

Scheduling & weather

Instructors monitor conditions and adjust the plan throughout the day. Unsafe conditions may mean shore-based learning, a delayed launch, or cancellation.

Let the program team know as soon as possible. Make-up availability depends on the session schedule and open space.

04

Equipment & clothing

Bring sun protection, a refillable water bottle, secure closed-toe footwear, and comfortable layers that can get wet.

No. Program boats and required safety equipment are provided. Families only need to supply personal clothing and daily essentials.

05

Registration

Start with prior experience and preferred learning style. Our Programs page compares all three options, and our team can help with the final choice.

Registration timing and availability will be published with each season. Contact the program team for the latest planning information.

06

Account access

The current Login and Sign up pages are front-end previews only. They do not create accounts or store credentials yet.

Password recovery is not active during the front-end preview. Use the Contact page if you need help with the future account experience.

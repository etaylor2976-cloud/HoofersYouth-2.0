Growing capable kids on the water.

Our mission

Give young people room to steer.

Sailing asks for attention, adaptability, and trust. We create a supportive environment where youth can try, adjust, and discover that they are more capable than they knew.

The goal is not just a better sailor. It is a young person who can meet a challenge, communicate with a crew, and make thoughtful decisions.

How we teach

Clear guidance. Real responsibility.

Young sailors grow when the next challenge feels possible—and when an instructor is close enough to help without taking over.

Safety

Safety is a daily practice.

It begins before anyone leaves the dock. Instructors review conditions, equipment, group readiness, and the day’s plan. Properly fitted life jackets, coach boats, clear boundaries, and weather awareness are part of every session.

  • Weather-aware decisions
  • Attentive, qualified instruction
  • Small-group communication
  • Age-appropriate independence

Our lake, our community

On Lake Mendota since 1963.

Generations of sailors have learned that the lake is both a classroom and a gathering place. We carry that community spirit forward by making room for new crews, new questions, and new adventures.

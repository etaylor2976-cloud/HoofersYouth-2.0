Our programs

Start where you are.
Go farther than you imagined.

01

Ages 10–18 · No experience needed

Beginner Sailing

Learn the essentials in a patient, upbeat crew where questions are encouraged and every new skill counts.

  • Boat parts and rigging
  • Steering and sail trim
  • Capsize confidence
  • On-water teamwork

Best fit: Curious first-time sailors ready to try something new.

02

Ages 10–18 · Prior sailing experience

Advanced Sailing

Sharpen technique, make smarter decisions on the water, and take on the thrill of friendly racing.

  • Advanced boat handling
  • Weather strategy
  • Racing fundamentals
  • Leadership on the water

Best fit: Returning sailors comfortable handling a boat.

03

Ages 10–18 · Beginner friendly

Windsurfing

Pair board balance with wind awareness for a dynamic, hands-on way to explore the lake.

  • Board balance
  • Rig control
  • Upwind and downwind travel
  • Safe independent practice

Best fit: Active learners excited by a physical challenge.

A typical session

Plenty of lake time. Just enough dock time.

Each day moves from a clear plan to hands-on practice, with time to reflect and celebrate progress.

  1. 01
    Meet & rig

    Check the weather, set a goal, and prepare the boats together.

  2. 02
    Learn & launch

    Practice one focused skill before taking it onto the water.

  3. 03
    Sail & explore

    Build confidence through games, challenges, and guided freedom.

  4. 04
    Dock & debrief

    Pack up, share wins, and choose the next skill to chase.

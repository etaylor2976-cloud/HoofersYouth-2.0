For parents & guardians

Good to know before you go.

Still curious? These are the questions we hear most from new sailing families.

No. Discover programs are designed for first-time sailors, and every level includes an age-appropriate skills progression.

Properly fitted life jackets, trained instructors, coach boats, small groups, and weather-aware plans are standard every day.

Bring sun protection, a refillable water bottle, secure closed-toe footwear, and comfortable clothes that can get wet.

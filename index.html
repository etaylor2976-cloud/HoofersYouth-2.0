Built for brave beginnings

Confidence comes with every tack.

Part of the Wisconsin Hoofers community, Hoofer Youth Sailing Club helps young people discover the lake through hands-on instruction, supportive coaching, and a welcoming crew. Sailors build practical skills, lasting confidence, and a connection to Madison’s sailing community.

Sailing AM

Sailing AM offers students the opportunity to hone their sailing and teambuilding skills on our RS Zests and Badger Techs. From day one, students will sail independently with a small crew of their peers. This course balances fun games and interactive lessons for a well rounded sailing experience.

Ages 13–172 weeks$5001:30 PM–4:30 PM

Keelboat Clinic

Prerequisites: Sailing Day Camp, Sailing AM, or Sailing PM

In this course, students will sail on one of the many Hoofer keelboats ranging from our 33ft racers to a relaxing sail on one of our cruisers. Students will sail among a small crew of young sailors taking turns skippering and crewing as a team led by our specialized keelboat instructors.

See more

Learning objectives:

  • Gain leadership and teamworking skills
  • Ability to sail all five points of sail
  • Tacking and gybing
  • Keelboat safety
  • Basic rigging and derigging of keelboats
  • Effective sail trim and use of sail controls
  • Handling larger vessels under sail and under engine
  • Proficiency in tying common knots

Intro to Sailing

Students will learn the fundamentals of sailing through hands-on instruction, interactive chalk talks, and on the water activities. Students start by sailing our Badger Sloops in small groups with their instructors, and progress to sailing our RS Zests or Badger Techs with their peers.

See more

Learning objectives:

  • Intro to rigging and derigging
  • Sail in any direction
  • Basic sail trim
  • Intro to boat parts
  • Safe capsize recovery
  • Tacking and gybing
  • Proper launching and landing on the pier
  • Basic knot tying

Advanced Day Camp

Prerequisites: Sailing Day Camp, Sailing AM, or Sailing PM.

Advanced Sailing Day Camp builds off the foundation set from prior courses. Students will have the opportunity to sail our more advanced 420 and Topaz Ranger fleets. During this course, students will learn to work with their crew to complete sailing challenges and participate in fun sailing regattas with their peers.
